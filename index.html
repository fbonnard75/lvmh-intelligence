Luxury Market Context

  • Global personal luxury goods growth forecast at 2.5–5% for 2026 (Morgan Stanley: ~2.5%; Bain: 3–5%; BNP Paribas: 6%), with caution prevailing due to macro and FX risks.
  • US remains the only region with clear positive catalysts, representing 23% of global luxury sales, supported by strong equity markets and wealth creation.
  • China stabilizing but structurally challenged: luxury sales expected broadly flat in 2026 as aspirational consumers retrench and younger demographics shift preferences.
  • A K-shaped dynamic is widening: ultra-high-net-worth spending resilient, while aspirational segment faces growing pressure from economic uncertainty.
  • Shift toward 'quiet luxury' and experiences (hospitality, fine dining, travel) at the expense of logo-driven hard goods accelerating across all markets.
  • Bernard Arnault increased family stake in LVMH to 50.01%, signaling conviction in long-term recovery despite a $50B year-to-date fortune decline.
