👜 Fashion & Leather Goods
-2% organic Q1 2026
Fashion & Leather Goods organic growth
  • LVMH Fashion & Leather Goods fell 2% organically in Q1 2026, impacted by the Middle East conflict which created a 1% drag on overall group revenue.
  • Christian Dior saw strong early reception for Jonathan Anderson's first designs, with Lady Dior reinterpretations performing exceptionally and the division 'improving quite a lot versus previous quarters.'
  • Louis Vuitton celebrated the 130th anniversary of its Monogram canvas, with new flagships in Beijing and Seoul driving strong performance; the brand is described as 'more resilient than average' in the division.
  • Celine faces challenges under new designer Michael Rider with a more commercial direction, while Givenchy also remains among the underperformers in the division.
🍾 Wines & Spirits
-9% organic Q4 2025 / -5% FY2025
Wines & Spirits organic revenue decline
  • Hennessy cognac revenue was 'held back by weaker local demand' due to customs duties issues in both China and the US, contributing to a 25% slide in division profit to €1.02bn for full year 2025.
  • Champagne houses Moët & Chandon and Dom Pérignon showed 'good resilience,' maintaining 22% combined market share despite broader sector challenges.
  • LVMH divested its Indian Chandon sparkling wine winery to Sula Vineyards, exiting local production in India after more than a decade of operations.
  • Moët Hennessy Champagne workers staged a strike in January 2026 after management confirmed the annual profit-sharing bonus would not be paid for the first time since 1968.
✨ Perfumes & Cosmetics
Stable organic, -6% reported Q1 2026
Perfumes & Cosmetics performance
  • Perfumes & Cosmetics revenue remained stable organically in Q1 2026, though down 6% on a reported basis amid currency headwinds.
  • Parfums Christian Dior performed well driven by new launches including J'adore Intense, Dior Addict eau de parfum, and the new Cuir Saddle signature scent in La Collection Privée.
  • Guerlain delivered strong growth led by its L'Art & La Matière and Aqua Allegoria fragrance collections, as well as the confirmed success of the iconic Rouge G lipstick.
  • The division maintained its 'robust innovation policy and highly selective retail approach,' with makeup innovations in Dior's Forever and Backstage lines also contributing positively.
💎 Watches & Jewelry
LVMH Watch Week 7th edition, Milan Jan 2026
Watches & Jewelry key event
  • LVMH Watch Week 2026 returned to Milan's Via Montenapoleone, showcasing nine houses including Bulgari, TAG Heuer, Zenith, Hublot, Louis Vuitton, Tiffany & Co., and L'Epée 1839.

🛍 Selective Retailing
+4% organic Q1 2026
Selective Retailing organic growth
  • Selective Retailing posted solid 4% organic growth in Q1 2026 with €4.05 billion in revenue, led by Sephora's continued market share gains across all regions.

Luxury Market Context

  • Global luxury goods market forecast to grow 3–5% in 2026 (Bain & Company), though Morgan Stanley is more cautious at ~2.5% growth amid slower-than-expected rebound.
  • K-shaped economy dynamics benefit ultra-luxury brands targeting HNWI, while aspirational segment brands face sustained pressure from cost-of-living concerns.
  • China luxury sales expected to remain broadly flat in 2026 as consumer tastes evolve and local brands gain ground, with LVMH flagging double-digit declines in the Middle East due to the Iran war.
  • North America remains a bright spot for luxury demand, supported by strong equity markets, wealth creation, and consistent consumer spending across income groups.
  • Bernard Arnault warned '2026 won't be simple' and is seeking shareholder approval to extend his tenure as Chairman & CEO to age 85, while the Arnault family approaches 50%+ of LVMH capital.
  • LVMH Q1 2026 overall revenue reached €19.1 billion (+1% organic), slightly below analyst consensus of +2%, reflecting continued macro headwinds and geopolitical disruption.
