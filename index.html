Luxury Market Context

  • Analyst consensus for 2026 personal luxury goods growth ranges from +2.5% (Morgan Stanley) to +6% (BNP Paribas), reflecting wide uncertainty due to macro, currency and geopolitical risks.
  • Bain & Company forecasts +3–5% luxury growth in 2026, contingent on US resilience backed by strong equity markets, steady European demand and gradual China recovery.
  • China is expected broadly flat to +6% in 2026; sentiment remains fragile but an affluent population of ~25 million is projected to gradually resume spending.
  • North America is the sector's current bright spot, with US consumer demand holding up despite economic uncertainty and benefiting from strong wealth-effect dynamics.
  • A 'tectonic shift' toward luxury experiences (hospitality, cruises, fine dining) over traditional goods is accelerating, reshaping category priorities across the sector.
  • Middle East conflict created a 1% drag on LVMH total group growth in Q1 2026, with regional sales down double digits — a risk that competitors are also navigating.
